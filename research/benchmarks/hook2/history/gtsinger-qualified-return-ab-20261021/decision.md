## Focused ablation and next test constraint

To identify which part of the combined experiment affected the independent Tenor clip, the same key-aware runner rendered its `Glissando_Group` with only the qualified-attack/phase-payback flags, and then with only the delayed-return-ratio flags. All 24 attack-only WAVs were byte-identical to the combined candidate, while all 24 return-only WAVs were byte-identical to the baseline. Thus the Tenor difference is caused by the attack branch; the return correction did not activate on this clip. The all-zero case has 1,237 changed stereo frames at 6.3028–6.3286 s, with unchanged measured F0 score. This is a real independent activation without demonstrated benefit—not a reason to narrow the rule by a Hook-2 timestamp or singer name.

The available GTSinger plan has more raw clips, including another Tenor glissando clip with a roughly sustained low note and brief semitone rise/return in the raw F0. A controlled F# Natural Minor Artist/Vibe comparison on that clip would test a legal target handoff, but the current macOS runner's fresh AudioUnit scan returned `No plug-in matches: Auto-Tune Artist`; its newly written catalog was empty. Seeding its catalog with the exact Artist AU description from a completed reference run changed the failure to `No compatible plug-in format exists for this plug-in`. The older calibration runner failed the same AU-format check, and `auval -a` did not list Artist. The calibration target was compiled with `JUCE_PLUGINHOST_AU=1`, so this is an AudioComponent discovery/registration failure in the current environment, **not** evidence that AU support was omitted from the build. Existing Artist WAVs use their original annotated keys and cannot be relabelled as an F# reference. No new Artist target was fabricated, no unverified DSP threshold was applied, and the candidate remains unpromoted.

Subsequent verification on 21 October narrows that environment failure: the user's normal macOS Terminal completed `auval -v aumf ATxL 'VST '` with **AU VALIDATION SUCCEEDED** and all render tests passed. ## Voiced-distance guard: tested and rejected

One narrow research guard required the singer's F0 to remain at least 30 cents below the newly committed target before enabling the qualified attack. It did not alter the flag-off Hook-2 baseline (still byte-identical to the selected app, WAV SHA-256 `1990aca4f64d96cc40abe4aedd3aadc932e6a551b234ccc851342ec23d88590d`), and the flag-on Hook-2 hard-tune WAV remained byte-identical to the prior qualified candidate (SHA-256 `b6961f7327b4418ce564f93330e3def94fbae40442225e450ff5d01c2dd95264`). On the Tenor Glissando technique job it removed 23 of the 24 changed cases, but the four-zero case still produced the **same** 1,237-frame change as before. A trace-enabled rerender nulled byte-for-byte against that four-zero candidate, so the trace itself did not create the event.

The Tenor host-control trace shows its new-target commitment at approximately source sample 301,536 (6.282 s), raw F0 about MIDI 55.66 versus target MIDI 56: a ~34-cent deficit. The comparable Hook-2 up commitment is near sample 227,680 (4.743 s), raw F0 214.619 Hz versus target 220 Hz: ~43 cents. Thus 30 cents admits both. A threshold between these two observations would have less than ten cents of separation and would be an unqualified, tracker-sensitive fit to two recordings. It was **not** promoted or left in the source. The research VST3 was rebuilt back to the original candidate SHA-256 `f44b260db8d665da761c91009de6ecb16857e0a7fdfd26680f0f07186524405b`; the selected listening app retains embedded VST3 SHA-256 `617682fa530632e3418793eb3cfb733b2a1359f36082672ce9237440935fbd20`.

The next plausible discriminator is the *predicted acoustic deficit of the already queued synthesis grains*, rather than a fixed F0-distance or singer/clip rule. That needs a direct, aligned baseline-versus-candidate output/epoch trace on multiple legally comparable gestures. Current evidence is insufficient to choose its threshold or verify that it remains stable under live F0 jitter; no new DSP release is justified yet.

## Synthesis-cadence deficit candidate (research only)

The source-period/epoch ledger gives a more direct state contrast than raw F0 distance. In the first new-target epochs, Hook-2's active synthesis cadence was still roughly 97 cents below the 220-Hz target, while the Tenor control around its affected handoff was about 46 cents below the 207.65-Hz target and soon reached the target. A new **diagnostic-only** condition therefore retains the original qualified attack only if the renderer's active synthesis cadence at target commitment is at least 60 cents below the target. It uses existing causal control state; it does not inspect Artist audio, song time, file name, singer identity, or MIDI note identity. It is not a public knob or production-default change.

The research source change is in `artifacts/crossing24-bounded-preview-20261017-112706/source/VibeAutotune/CausalTDPSOLARenderer.h`, after `synthesisFrequency` is computed. The rebuilt uninstalled VST3 binary SHA-256 is `5f7aa1becf3367a2a09aea63ff7789580b406f04335ae19f331ec627dee07a33`. With diagnostic flags off, its Hook-2 hard-tune render still has the selected-app WAV SHA-256 `1990aca4f64d96cc40abe4aedd3aadc932e6a551b234ccc851342ec23d88590d`. With the qualified-attack/phase-payback and delayed-return flags on, all 24 Hook-2 case WAVs are byte-identical to the prior ungated candidate; in particular the hard-tune-zero SHA remains `b6961f7327b4418ce564f93330e3def94fbae40442225e450ff5d01c2dd95264`. Thus this gate did not erase the previously measured Hook-2 benefit.

The same exact StudioForge host rendered all 12 GTSinger jobs and 24 control cases each against the pinned Artist references. The new full run is [`cadence-gate-full`](cadence-gate-full). Compared with the unchanged baseline, 32/288 Vibe WAVs differ, versus 47/288 for the ungated candidate. All 12 hard-tune-zero WAVs now null byte-for-byte against baseline, including the formerly affected Tenor Glissando 6.30-s event. The 32 differences are confined to nonzero-control cases in the two Tenor Glissando jobs (13 Control, 19 Glissando); the other ten jobs remain entirely byte-identical.

Across all 288 reports, using the same 0.05-cent diagnostic threshold for median F0 error against Artist: 11 improved, 3 worsened, 274 were effectively unchanged; maximum worsening is 0.107 cents and maximum improvement 0.239 cents. Five cases changed transition count. In the 32 changed WAVs, a coarse maximum adjacent-sample jump check over each modified interval found no increased local maximum; this does **not** establish click-free or formant-preserving perception. Four focused tests pass: `VibeAutotuneCausalTDPSOLATest`, `VibeAutotuneCorrectionTrajectoryTest`, `VibeAutotuneLiveControlPipelineTest`, and `VibeAutotuneKeyScaleLivePathTest`.

**Decision: retain as a narrower research candidate, not a product build.** It removes a demonstrated no-benefit activation at the requested hard-tune-zero setting without losing Hook-2's measured improvement, and it reduces collateral changes for other knobs. But the 60-cent cutoff is supported by only two distinct event contexts; the available Artist dataset still lacks a second comparable legal low-register adjacent rise/return on which to demonstrate positive transfer. Live callback/CPU, formant, sibilance and listening qualifications remain outstanding. The user's selected app VST3 SHA-256 remains `617682fa530632e3418793eb3cfb733b2a1359f36082672ce9237440935fbd20`, unchanged and un-repackaged.

## New independent Artist render: cadence gate does not transfer

The user's normal Terminal subsequently validated the Artist AU and rendered a new F# Natural Minor reference for an independent Tenor glissando clip. StudioForge rendered both Vibe baseline and cadence-gated candidate on that exact clip and key. The candidate changed its hard-tune-zero output for only 954 samples near raw-clock 2.175–2.195 s, but local mean/P90 pitch error against Artist increased (102.079→102.211 c mean; 127.362→136.345 c P90). Whole-clip median error stayed 88.758 c. Across 24 knob cases, 3 whole-clip median scores improved, 6 worsened and 15 were within 0.05 c. This is not positive transfer; **do not promote the candidate or change the selected DAW app**. Full settings, provenance and caveats: [`independent-fsharp/decision.md`](independent-fsharp/decision.md).

The exact-host traces then identified a causal-state discriminator: Hook 2 had a confirmed renderer crossing into the new adjacent note immediately before commit; this Tenor event did not. A further *diagnostic-only* crossing requirement keeps the improved Hook-2 hard-tune WAV byte-identical while returning all 24 Tenor F# cases to baseline. Across all 288 original-key GTSinger renders it is byte-identical to baseline. Four focused tests pass and flag-off Hook-2 controls null against baseline. This removes the known false activation but **does not yet prove positive transfer or justify packaging**. See the last section of [`independent-fsharp/decision.md`](independent-fsharp/decision.md).
